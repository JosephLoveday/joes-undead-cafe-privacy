Privacy Policy - Joe

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Joes Undead Cafe does not collect, store on a server, share or sell anypersonal information. The game works entirely offline.

What the game stores

Your cafe progress, settings and any files you choose to save (such as abug report file) are written to the app's private storage on your own device. They never leave your device unless you copy or share them yourself. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Error log and feedback

The game keeps a small error log in its private storage (script errors and warnings only; addresses, file paths and long ids are stripped). Nothingleaves your device unless you choose "Send feedback", which copies a text report to your clipboard and saves it on the device. You decide whether and where to send it. There is no automatic upload.

What the game does not do

  • It requests no internet access and makes no network connections.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or trackingsoftware.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • It does not create accounts.

The only permission the Android app declares is vibration, used for hapticfeedback.

Children

Because the game collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds online features, ads or purchases, this policywill be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
